What can go wrong

  • Issuer risk: tokens are claims on assets held by the issuer — you carry counterparty exposure to that company.
  • Price risk: stocks, gold, and bond funds move down as well as up. Yield on Treasury products is variable and never promised.
  • Self-custody responsibility: only you hold the keys — which also means recovering from mistakes is on you. The course teaches the safety steps in order.
  • No deposit insurance: these are not bank deposits and are not covered by deposit-protection schemes.
